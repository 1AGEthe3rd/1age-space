# Founder direction: Global Property Index & Creative Spaces

Accepted November 21, 2026. Applies to subsequent 1AGE Space builds; implementation status is reported separately.

1AGE Space is an independent, immersive 3D platform for commercial buildings, arts/creative spaces, and compatible virtual properties. It is not a social network or a vacancy-only directory. Permanent buildings may be indexed whether occupied, available, unknown, or off-market. Prioritize Portland, Oregon and Vancouver, Washington, then expand nationally and globally. Vancouver, British Columbia data does not fulfill the Vancouver, Washington pilot.

## Records and discovery

Keep Building, Space/Unit, Listing, Business/Tenant, Virtual Space, and Source as distinct linked entities. Index a building once; do not create another building for each tenant, room, studio, or changing listing. Explicit primary building addresses and separately identified units require evidence; do not automatically split ambiguous address strings or turn every existing location into a verified building.

Cover retail, office/coworking, warehouse/industrial, restaurants/hospitality, mixed-use and development sites. Creative coverage includes galleries, museums/cultural centers, artist studios, photography/film, music recording/rehearsal, fashion/design, makerspaces, creative coworking, arts complexes, and legally permitted live/work.

Temporary discovery includes pop-ups, exhibitions, short-term galleries, hourly studios/rehearsal rooms, events/performance, production locations, vendors, residencies, and daily/weekly/monthly rentals. Support hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, and long-term durations only where evidenced.

Search should grow to include city/neighborhood/address/name, category and discipline, status, duration, rate, size/capacity, amenities, physical/virtual distinction, and verified listings. Unknown must remain distinct from verified availability. Equipment, accessibility, permitted activities, and lease/use rights need explicit records; do not infer them from a venue name.

## Sources and permissions

Investigate Craigslist, Zillow, LoopNet, assessor/public-property records, municipal open data, authorized feeds, owner submissions, operators, and arts directories. Public visibility does not establish redistribution rights. Do not scrape restricted listing services without authorization. Track source, retrieval date, licence/terms, permitted fields, source version, and verification status. Existing municipal records remain source-attributed and may be historical.

Claims require authenticated participants, proof of ownership/management authority, and scoped approval. A tenant's unit claim cannot grant building-wide control. Future publishing must distinguish draft, submitted, reviewed, verified, rejected, and expired states. No local demo claim is an actual verification.

## 3D and ecosystem

Keep the virtual environment primary, with practical accessible information panels and fallback. Support geographic discovery, selectable buildings/units, authorized interiors, and creator-published environments. Generic geometry must remain labeled as a template; it is not a surveyed replica or proof of owner permission. Creators retain underlying rights under applicable agreements.

Connect to Search, Page, World, Games, Exchange, and Cash through documented interfaces. Each application remains independently accessible. Cash stays an internal credit distinct from cryptocurrency; payment custody, conversion, rental settlement, and other financial operations need separately approved designs.

## Delivery order

1. Audit existing source, deployment, scenes, datasets, and contracts; preserve recoverable baselines.
2. Build the Portland/Vancouver WA permanent property foundation and address deduplication.
3. Add separately evidenced units, creative categories, temporary listings, and filters.
4. Connect records to performant 3D exploration and authorized virtual spaces.
5. Add secure owner/manager participation, verification, scoped publishing, and listing maintenance.
6. Expand lawful sources and cross-platform search/integration nationally and globally.

Do not restart the application, silently overwrite records, or describe prototypes/source checks as completed production integrations. Report tested counts, actual functionality, remaining gaps, and reproduction instructions for each build.
