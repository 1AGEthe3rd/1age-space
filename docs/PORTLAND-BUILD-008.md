# Kenton station to N Denver Avenue

On the Portland review build, ride the Yellow Line to **Kenton/N Denver Ave** and leave through the platform doors. Walk away from the train along the original virtual Denver Avenue block. The named intersections, in order from the platform, are N McClellan, N Kilpatrick and N Schofield. The central doorway between McClellan and Kilpatrick opens into a 1AGE Design Studio concept with display shelves. Walk back to the open train door to board and continue in either direction.

The route is continuous in the rail scene. The six storefront exteriors and studio interior are procedural interpretations. The design studio is a virtual studio concept. This build adds zero physical property, lease or occupancy records. The streetscape uses compressed distances. The station, the modeled platforms and the MLK driving scene have separate walking and driving boundaries.
